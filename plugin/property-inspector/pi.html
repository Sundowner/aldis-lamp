Pick a signal and it goes straight into the phrase, with its meaning here. WW2 meanings come from B.R. 232 Signal Card (1937), U.S. FTP 223A (1945) and the British-US BUSCI convoy instructions; the last two groups are modern ICS and unsourced folklore, and are labelled as such.
Choosing a signal adds it to the phrase straight away, saves it and retitles the key. Add again repeats the one you picked. Unknown characters are skipped.
With the box unticked and the title field left empty, the key reads ≡ HEAVE TO instead of ≡ HEAVE TO 12w. A title you type yourself always wins.
Leave it empty and the key recharges for exactly as long as the send takes; the ring on the key counts that down. Type a number of seconds to force a longer wait. Presses while the ring is still running are ignored.
Default L. Star Citizen toggles the lights with one key; every lit element sends it twice (on, then off).
